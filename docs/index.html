How I work

Define the task.
Direct the build.
Check the result.

I’m interested in the space between an operational problem and a tool someone can actually use. My background in import/export documentation makes missing information, exceptions and traceability familiar concerns.

I put AI to work across research, implementation, debugging and documentation. I focus on choosing a useful problem, guiding the work, checking the output and refining the details that make the result usable.

Direction & judgment

Define who the tool serves, what it needs to do and how to assess the result. Use feedback to decide what needs changing.

Execution & delivery

Direct AI through the build, work through failures and prepare usable demos, recorded checks and clear instructions.
